# Scroll brief

Self-authored, not interviewed (run 4, 2026-09-27; Colton was not available
and the run was told not to wait). Written in the brand's voice from
`brand/BRAND.md`, `docs/DESIGN-DECISIONS.md` and the shipped copy. It answers
the eight questions the scroll-craft skill asks before any scroll work, so
the next run that touches motion or media (the video run, the Higgsfield
run) starts from a stated intent instead of a guess. Nothing here is a fact
about VKC; it is a statement of how the pages should feel.

## 1. Vibe, in five words

Printed label. Sealed floor. Nothing sold.

References, not sites: a pallet label read under a plant's lights; a QC sheet
filled in ballpoint; the fill line moulded into a jug.

## 2. The scroll journey, section by section (home page)

1. The tagline and the drawing: two containers filled to one line.
2. The two services, as two labels.
3. Which one fits: two questions, one answer.
4. What has run: the container families, one label, three fields.
5. Where we focus: the trades, as a manifest.
6. How a first job starts: three ticked steps.
7. Straight talk: what the site will not say.
8. What a plant manager asks first.
9. Tell us what is backing up (the negative band), then the footer.

## 3. The energy curve

Calm at the top (the drawing does the talking), a lift at the chooser (the
reader acts), flat and factual through containers and industries, one quiet
moment at "Straight talk", and a hard, held close on the negative band.

## 4. The feeling curve, and the one moment

| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Tagline and gauge | Recognition | Their own line, drawn as a fill line |
| Two labels | Clarity | Yours or ours, no third thing |
| Chooser | Agency | They pick, the page answers |
| Containers, industries | Trust | Lists that say what is known and nothing more |
| Steps | Ease | Three things, in order |
| Straight talk | Relief | A page that says what it will not claim |
| Questions | Reassurance | Their questions, already answered |
| Negative band | Resolve | The darkest thing on the page, and the one action |

The peak is the negative band: the page holds its breath and asks for one
thing. The tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where the whole page fills
to the line as you read it, and then it turns the label over."

## 5. One thing no other site does

The header's rule fills from its tick to the edge as the page is read. It is
the brand's one device used as the scroll's own gauge, in CSS only, and it is
gone (only the tick stands) under reduced motion.

## 6. How far from premium-minimal

Not far. The range word is "printed": one accent, hairlines and ink rules,
mono field names, no shadows, no gradient, no imagery until Colton supplies
it. Brutalist in its refusal to decorate, not in its type.

## 7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes

Distinct scenes. Each section is a label on the floor; the scroll walks
from one to the next. There is no camera and no continuous flight, so a
later media run adds covers into the reserved slots (`content/scenes.json`)
without changing the page's structure.

## 8. What exists already

The wordmark, the six tokens, the two faces, the fill-rule device, the hero
drawing, the pictograms. No photographs, no footage, no client marks. The
media seam is `content/scenes.json` plus `public/media/`, empty until Colton
fills it (CLAUDE.md §1 still bans generated imagery; NEEDS-COLTON item 8).

## Authored silence

The floor between sections is empty on purpose. A section that has nothing
to add to the feeling curve was cut in run 1 (`docs/DESIGN-DECISIONS.md` §5);
none of the gaps above is dead scroll waiting for content.
